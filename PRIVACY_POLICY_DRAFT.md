# HOGO (CampusRide) — Privacy Policy (Draft for Production)

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Applicability:** All users of the HOGO (CampusRide) mobile applications and associated online platforms.  

---

## 1. Introduction & Scope
HOGO ("CampusRide", "we", "us", or "our") operates a peer-to-peer campus ride-pooling community exclusively for verified college students, faculty, and academic staff. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, processing, storage, and protection of personal data in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** of India and applicable telecommunication safety guidelines.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

### 2.1 Information Provided Directly by You
- **Identity & Contact Details:** Full legal name, institutional email address (`@college.ac.in`), personal phone number, University Seat Number (USN), and emergency contact phone numbers.
- **Verification & KYC Documents:** College Identity Card, Government Identity (Aadhaar or Driving License), and a live verification selfie.
- **Vehicle Information (For Providers):** Vehicle Registration Number (RC), model/make, vehicle type (Bike, Car, SUV, XUV), and vehicle registration images.

### 2.2 Data Collected Automatically During App Usage
- **Real-Time Geospatial Coordinates:** Precise GPS location collected exclusively during an active ride to enable navigation, safety tracking, and SOS emergency dispatch.
- **Ride Telemetry:** Pickup and drop coordinates, departure and arrival times, and completed route traces.
- **In-App Communications:** In-ride chat messages exchanged between riders and drivers during active bookings.
- **Device & Technical Identifiers:** Push notification tokens (Expo FCM/APNs), hardware device UUID, and operating system build.

---

## 3. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Processing
We process your personal information strictly for legitimate mobility and campus safety purposes:
1. **Service Delivery:** Matching seekers with verified campus providers going in the same direction.
2. **Identity Verification & Safety:** Ensuring only authentic, non-suspended college members access campus rides.
3. **Emergency SOS & Incident Response:** Dispatching live GPS coordinates to emergency contacts, campus security, and emergency responders upon SOS activation.
4. **Fraud & Abuse Prevention:** Enforcing single-device session integrity and preventing account takeover.

---

## 4. Document Access and Masking Safeguards
- **Zero Public Access to KYC:** Identity documents uploaded for verification are stored in secured, access-controlled cloud repositories. They are never exposed on public student profiles or search queries.
- **Access Control:** Only verified institutional campus administrators and the account owner can view submitted identification documents.
- **In-Ride Contact Masking:** Personal contact numbers are concealed until a ride booking is accepted.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Erasure Policy
- **Account Profiles:** Retained for the duration of the student's active enrollment or until account deletion is requested.
- **Ride GPS Telemetry:** Retained for 30 days following ride completion for safety review, then purged.
- **KYC Verification Assets:** Retained during active platform membership plus 90 days following account closure for audit and regulatory compliance.
- **Emergency SOS Logs:** Retained for 180 days to support institutional safety evaluations and law enforcement inquiries where mandated.

---

## 6. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023
As a data principal, you have the following rights:
1. **Right to Access & Review:** View all personal information and KYC verification statuses stored in your profile.
2. **Right to Correction:** Update inaccurate phone numbers, vehicle details, or emergency contacts.
3. **Right to Erasure:** Request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data upon graduation or discontinuance of service.
4. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Lodge safety or privacy inquiries with our designated Data Protection Officer.
